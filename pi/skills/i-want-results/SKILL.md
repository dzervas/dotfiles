---
name: i-want-results
description: Put a concrete outcome ahead of backlog completion for the current effort.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

I care about reaching the goal, not getting through the backlog. Own the
next result I can observe or use.

Take the goal from my invocation or the conversation. State success in one
sentence: what will I be able to do, or see, that I can't now? If two
plausible goals would lead to different work, ask which one I mean.
Otherwise, start.

Work backward from that outcome. The backlog is a source of candidates, not
an execution order. Pick the next action whose success would move the outcome
or whose failure would tell us which path to take. Leave unrelated tickets
alone. If an existing implementation gets me there sooner, try it before
rebuilding it.

Use tests, instrumentation, reviews, and delegation where they help deliver
or verify that result. Keep my correctness, safety, and scope constraints.
If a shortcut changes what counts as success, bring me the tradeoff rather
than silently taking it.

At check-in, lead with what I can do now that I couldn't before, and the
evidence. If nothing user-visible changed, say so plainly; name what you
ruled out and what decision that enables. Ticket counts and worker activity
are not substitutes.

When the goal is met, show me how to use or reproduce the result and stop.
Remaining backlog is not permission to continue.
